Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly is built to help you track your personal expenses with as little friction as possible. To provide this service, we collect only the information needed to run your account.

This may include:

  • Account information: your email address and a password you choose when signing up.
  • Expense data: the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you record.
  • Session data: basic technical information needed to keep you signed in.

We do not collect your bank account numbers, card details, or any other financial credentials. Spendly does not connect to your bank or payment providers.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide exclusively to operate Spendly for you. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Authenticate you when you sign in.
  • Store, retrieve, and display the expenses you log.
  • Generate the category breakdowns and monthly summaries you request.
  • Send essential service notifications, such as password reset emails.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers using industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Access to your account is protected by your password, which is hashed and never stored in plain text.

You can delete your account and all associated expense data at any time from your profile settings. Doing so permanently removes your records from our systems and is not reversible.

While we take reasonable measures to safeguard your information, no system is completely secure. You acknowledge that you provide your data at your own risk.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of trusted third-party services to operate. These providers only receive the minimum data needed to perform their function and are bound by their own privacy and security obligations.

Current third-party services include:

  • Hosting and infrastructure providers that store and serve Spendly's data.
  • Transactional email providers used to send you password resets and important account notices.
  • Analytics tools that help us understand aggregate usage patterns so we can improve the product.

We periodically review these providers to ensure they meet our standards for data protection.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. You can update most of your account information directly within Spendly, or contact us to assist with any request.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Spendly after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the channels listed on our website.
